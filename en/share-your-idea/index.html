Basic & Beyond proposal privacy notice

The controller is Domenico De Luca, operator of RendimentoBB; privacy contact: support@rendimentobb.com. We process the problem, description, preferences, language and optional contact to assess your proposal and provide any requested reply, based on your specific submission consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Providing data is optional; without consent for this purpose we cannot receive the form, without affecting your property account. Marketing has additional, optional and separate consent. This form sends no emails, activates no newsletter and does not verify address ownership. Marketing preferences remain pending verification and do not automatically authorise a campaign.

Requests are stored in Firebase and accessible to the operator and authorised technical providers, subject to the general Privacy information on recipients and transfers. They are not published. Operational retention: up to 90 days, with removal in the next available cleanup cycle; backup copies follow their rotation. For security, we temporarily process pseudonymous network-address and, if provided, email identifiers to limit submissions, based on the legitimate interest in preventing abuse (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Duplicate-prevention keys last 24 hours; abuse counters up to 48 hours, plus the next available cleanup cycle.

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw consent through the privacy contact. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. You may complain to the Italian supervisory authority. A proposal can become a general roadmap theme only after review and removal of personal or confidential information. No automated decisions with legal effects are intended.
