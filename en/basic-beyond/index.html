We started with a real problem.

Understanding an investment and managing it takes more than one number. RendimentoBB connected simulations, properties, bookings and reports. We now want to bring this approach to other needs: clarify, choose, verify and improve.

The problem first. Then the tools.

  1. 01 · UnderstandWhat do you want to improve? Which repetitive task or missing information is holding you back?
  2. 02 · ChooseDefine goals, experience and constraints. An external tool may also be the right choice.
  3. 03 · BuildDesign a bounded solution and verify its usefulness. This stage is still being designed.
  4. 04 · SupportReview use, maintenance and improvements. A future journey shaped by real needs.
