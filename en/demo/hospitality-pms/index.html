Tourist tax

Fictional rule: €2 per taxable guest per night, up to 5 nights. This is not a verified municipal rate. In the real product configure the property rule and check exemptions and regulations.

For exemption set taxable guests to zero. No money is collected or remitted.

What this demo represents

Investor and Pro include property and booking management, calendar and operational checklists. Pro adds integrated reports and IT/EN PDFs. This demo reproduces a workflow with fictional data, not every screen or integration. It does not import bookings or access an account.
